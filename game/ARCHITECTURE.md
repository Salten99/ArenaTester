# Architecture

How the game code is organized. The first part is for you (the developer); the rest is the map Claude reads at the start of every session. Keep it current when systems change.

## In plain English

**The game:** pick a race, a weapon and a god, then travel a valley full of tribes and wild gods while Count Fuckula's corrupted tribe spreads. Each fight can reveal a god: pledge to them (new element and abilities, permanent; your weapon stays) or take a godly gift. Win the final battle or die; either way you unlock things for the next run.

**How a run flows today:**
1. Start screen (the *run director*): pick race, weapon, god, length.
2. The game generates the valley from a random seed (who lives where, who is corrupted, which gods are wild).
3. You walk the 3D valley (2.3 x 4.6 km, Count Fuckula at the north end). Walking into a tribe's village starts its event (a raid, a siege, a champion's challenge or a camp to clear); winning it makes them allies. Tribes are never conquered. Other encounters are places: walk into a camp and its fight starts, or press F at a shrine; the enemies attack right there. Your tribe's warriors (and your allies') join every fight.
4. Winning gives XP and a god offer (pledge or godly gift). Then you keep exploring.
5. Meanwhile the evil tribe's clock pushes into regions; you can defend them (Border Defense).
6. Final battle won = victory. Death = defeat. Unlocks are saved to your profile.

**What you edit vs. what Claude edits:**

| You | Ask Claude |
|---|---|
| `Game_Data_Template.xlsx` (gods, weapons, godly gifts, races, units, regions, numbers), then run `tools\export_data.bat` | Any `.gd` script |
| `tuning/combat_tuning.tres` in the Inspector (feel, XP speed, pledge penalty) | New weapon types, godly gift effects, god passives, unlock rules |
| Models in `game/models/` (.glb from Blender) | Hooking models up (`ModelRegistry`) |
| `docs/PLAYTEST.md` notes after you play | Fixing anything a test or playtest finds |

## Glossary

- **Milestone 0 / M0:** the combat arena prototype (now "Practice arena"). **Milestone 1 / M1:** one short run start to finish.
- **Run length 0.34:** a short run, about a third of a full one (fewer tribes, faster clock).
- **Godly Gift:** a god's power-up (one per offer). **Duo godly gift:** needs godly gifts from two specific gods. **Fire + Lightning:** the duo godly gift of the Lord of the Forge and the Storm Caller (lightning explodes burning enemies).
- **Pledge:** switch to a new god for the rest of the run. **Legacy bonus:** what's left of your old god's godly gifts (a damage %).
- **Void / hollowed:** the evil tribe's mark; 5 stacks switch your godly gifts off for 3 seconds.
- **sam-movement:** your movement controller project, ported in.
- **Autoload:** a script Godot keeps running for the whole game (like `Run` or `GameData`). **`.tres`:** a Godot settings file you edit in the Inspector. **Headless:** running the game without a window (how the tests run). **`user://`:** `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Pantheon Valley\`.
- **Co-op rules 1 and 2:** input separate from logic, and seeded randomness, so co-op can be added later.

| `RunRng` | autoload/run_rng.gd | The only gameplay randomness, seeded per run. |
| `HitStop` | autoload/hit_stop.gd | Brief game slowdown on impact (real-time timed). |

| `Run` | autoload/run.gd | The run in progress: `start_new`, `resume`, `tick` (evil clock; paused while `in_fight`), `finish_encounter`, `checkpoint`, `end`. Holds `Run.state` (a `RunState`) and `Run.player`. Saves on window close. |
| `Playtest` | autoload/playtest.gd | Playtest numbers (Council V): session and run time, time to first fight, each fight's length vs `target_minutes`, deaths, kills, regions entered. | `RetroLook` | autoload/retro_look.gd | The retro look, crisp pixels (user, 2026-11-09; PS2 style before, PS1 first): a CanvasLayer at layer -1 with `shaders/retro_screen.gdshader`, which redraws the 3D world at 270 square-pixel lines with 48 shades and a light dot pattern (`softness` 1 and 448 lines give the old soft PS2 look). `RetroLook.paint(material)` gives a model's material sharp texture pixels and, if it has no texture, pixel grit (a 32 px light-and-dark texture laid over it in 6 cm pixels); `CharacterModel.use_vertex_colors` calls it for every model not under `models/own`, and the valley for its ground, roads and plants; HUDs and menus sit on higher layers and stay sharp. Lighting is part of the look too: no sun shadows (each `CharacterModel` and `BeastModel` gets a dark disc decal at its feet, `add_blob_shadow`), plain colors (linear tone mapping), a soft PS2 glow on bright things (`ValleyWorld._ps2_glow`, also the arena) and closer valley fog (`fog_density` in `ValleyWorld._build_environment`). |

## Rules (keep these)

1. **Input is separate from logic.** The player reads a `PlayerIntent` each physics frame from `input_source` (keyboard via `PlayerInput`, scripted input in tests, the network later). Gameplay code never reads `Input` directly. *(Co-op rule 1.)*
2. **Seeded randomness.** Gameplay uses `RunRng` (or a `RandomNumberGenerator` seeded from the run seed, as `ValleyGenerator` and `EncounterSystem` do). Visual-only effects may use the globals. *(Co-op rule 2.)*
3. **Game time, not wall time.** Godly Gift cooldowns and timed buffs use `Combat.now_msec()`. Only `HitStop` uses the real clock.
4. **All damage goes through `Combat.deal()`.** Build a `DamageInfo` and hand it over. `DamageInfo.Kind` stops godly gifts re-triggering on their own damage.
5. **Data comes from the spreadsheet.** Numbers live in `Game_Data_Template.xlsx` -> JSON. Feel and progression numbers live in `combat_tuning.tres`. Anything the sheet can't express yet goes in a small, commented table in code (`GodlyGiftRules.RULES`, `ModelRegistry`). `DataCheck.run()` cross-checks the sheets on load (ids exist, region borders are two-way, exactly one home); its problems print as `Spreadsheet:` warnings and fail a test.
6. **Run state is plain data, and it owns the build.** Everything that must survive a save lives in `RunState` and goes through `to_dict()`/`from_dict()` (with `migrate()` for old `VERSION`s). `GodlyGiftManager` writes godly gifts back to `state.godly_gifts`; `Player.apply_run_state(state)` rebuilds the player from it. World systems are static functions over a `RunState`, with no scene access, so they're testable and network-friendly.
7. **Scenes stay minimal; nodes are built in code.** Claude can edit and test everything as text. Models come from `ModelRegistry`; the capsule is the fallback.

## Damage flow

```
Player attack ──> WeaponMoveset.light_hit/heavy_hit
               ──> Player.hit_targets() or fire_projectile()  ──> Player.make_hit() (race % + god % + godly_gift % + crit)
               ──> Combat.deal(enemy, info)
                     ├─ enemy.take_hit()         health, knockback, stagger
                     ├─ statuses applied          StatusEffects.apply()
                     ├─ hit-stop, shake, number   feedback
                     ├─ signals: hit_landed, element_hit, status_applied, killed
                     └─ Lightning from the player chains to 2 (+chain_bonus) enemies
Player ──> GodlyGiftManager.trigger(event, ctx)   twice per swing:
             phase "target" once per enemy hit (statuses, single-target damage)
             phase "swing"  once per swing (area blasts, buffs)
```

## Godly Gifts

**Classes** (2026-11-09): a class is its melee weapon. The class columns on the Weapons sheet (`class_name`, the `_mod_pct` stats, `armor_pct`, `stealth_pct`) become the `class` `PlayerStats` source in `Player.equip`; `stealth_pct` shrinks `Enemy._notice_distance`. C or the wheel only switches to the sidearm; chests offer only the other sidearm. **Class looks per race** (2026-11-17): every race has the same classes; `ModelRegistry.class_part(race, class, part)` finds your `own/<race>/<class>/body|weapon|shield.glb` and `race_part(race, name)` your `own/<race>/bow|staff|knife.glb`. `for_race(race, class)` and `weapon_model` try those first, then the old lookups; `Player.equip` rebuilds the body when the class has its own (`_body`). `own()` also finds people files in `own/<race>/people/` (`_own_people`, `rescan()` after an import). The art checklist groups them as `Race: <name>`. Blender sources live in `art_src/`, outside the Godot project. **Guardian parry** (2026-11-17): `Player.parries()` (Guardian class, main weapon out) turns the dodge key into `State.PARRY`; `take_hit` asks `_parries_hit` (enemy hit, from the front, within `parry_window`); a melee blow calls `Enemy.parried()` (STAGGER for `parry_stagger`) and `_parry` adds the riposte buffs with `PlayerStats.add_timed`; a hit with `DamageInfo.shot` (set by `Projectile`) is only blocked. The shield is `gear_shield.glb` (`make_weapons.py`) on `handslot.l` via `CharacterModel.set_offhand`; the greatsword file is no longer `TWO_HANDED`.

`GodlyGiftManager` (under the player) owns godly gifts, ranks them up, makes offers (rarity-weighted, plus a duo godly gift once both gods are in the build) and runs effects by `effect_type`: Apply Status, Bonus Damage, Area Blast (or a lingering `Hazard`), Projectile, Stat Buff, Heal, Shield, Knockback/Pull. Passive godly gifts become a `PlayerStats` source. `suppress(seconds)` switches every godly gift off (the Void mark). Godly gifts the code can't run yet (effect type not handled, a trigger that never fires such as on_block, or a stat nobody reads: `PlayerStats.READ_STATS`) are never offered: `GodlyGiftManager.is_implemented()` filters offers and duos, `unbuilt()` lists them, and GameData prints them at startup. Level-ups are filtered the same way (`LevelSystem.is_implemented`). `load_from(state)` restores godly gifts from a save.

Triggers: the sheet's `trigger_event` plus `on_element_hit` (code-only, used by Fire + Lightning).

## Weapons and abilities

- **Weapons** are picked with the race (`RunState.weapon_id`, Weapons sheet; `GameData.weapon_options(race)` reads the race's `weapon_options`, blank = all). `Player.weapon()` is the weapon's row (stats, class, attack category); `Player.element()` is the pledged god's element.
- **5 weapon movesets** (`WeaponMoveset.for_class(weapon_class)`): **Heavy** (3-hit combo, 4 m ground slam), **Blade** (wide arcs, spin finisher, dash-slash heavy), **Polearm** (line thrusts, sweep finisher, 360° heavy), **Ranged** (arrows, 3-arrow finisher, piercing heavy), **Caster** (element bolts, triple-bolt finisher, blast at range). Each also names its animations.
- `RaceActive` runs your race's active skill (V): one branch per race in `cast()`, numbers from the Races sheet. A race with no branch gets a plain blast.
- `AbilityRunner` runs any god's 3 abilities from the God Abilities columns. **Shapes:** the sheet's `shape` column set to Cone hits a cone ahead (`range_m` long, `radius_m` wide each side at the end); a Movement cone lunges you `lance_lunge_m` forward first and plays your `<ability_id>.tscn` (anywhere under `models/own/fx/`) where the cone bursts out (Fire Lance, `forge_fire_lance`). The row's `animation_name` plays if the rig has that clip (else Throw). **Aimed ultimates:** holding the key sets `PlayerIntent.ultimate_held` and `aim_point` (the camera's ground point, `PlayerInput._camera_ground_point`); `Player._update_aim` shows a ring at `aim_target()` (clamped to `AbilityRunner.aim_range()`) and casts on release with `cast(slot, player, dir, at)`. Scripted input with no aim point lands ahead (ranged ultimates) or where you stand. **Every ultimate** goes through `UltimateFx.play(element, spot, radius)` (`combat/ultimate_fx.gd`): a `MagicCircle` node (two counter-spinning layers of flat line ribbons built with SurfaceTool, tilted to the ground normal under the spot) at `ult_circle_height`, then after `ult_circle_delay` the element's strike falls for `ult_fall_time` (`_strike`: meteor, storm, icicles, shards, tornado, gas bomb + cloud, laser). `AbilityRunner` deals the damage once at `UltimateFx.impact_time()` (`_blast`, or `_multi_hit` with no radius, its hits coming from the circle); your own `<ability_id>.tscn` still plays at the spot on cast and then only the circle is drawn. The player holds `Ranged_Magic_Raise` for `ult_raise_time` unless the rig has the row's `animation_name`.
- God passives are a `match god_id` in `Player._god_passive_on_hit` (only Fire Passive so far).
- Pledging: `PledgeSystem.pledge_state(state, god)` changes only the run state; `pledge(state, player, god)` also re-syncs the player. It swaps god, element, abilities and passive (the weapon and moveset stay); the old god's godly gifts become a legacy damage bonus (tuning: `legacy_godly_gifts_kept`, `legacy_damage_per_godly_gift`).

## Movement

`Player._move()` is the sam-movement controller ported to the intent system: walk/sprint, fading momentum, speed-scaled jumps, a speed-adding slide, wall-jumps, crouch and a speed cap. Constants are in the tuning resource. The dodge (Ctrl) is separate from the slide; dodging also clears one Void stack.

## Models and animation

`CharacterModel` loads a rigged model, merges the KayKit Rig_Medium animation files into one `AnimationLibrary` per skeleton shape (rigs share one only if every `POSITION_BONES` bone rests within a centimetre; the animations' position keys for hips, shoulders, hip joints and hand slots are refitted to the model's own bone lengths, so any proportions work), walks and gallops four extra leg bones in code when a model has them (`quad_*`: the centaur, the mammoth), keeps a rider's posed hips on the saddle (`set_seat`), attaches the weapon to the `handslot.r` bone, and tints the model through a material overlay (hit flash, telegraph, burning, Void). **Your own models:** `ModelRegistry.own(file)` returns `models/own/<file>` when the sheet's model file exists there; every lookup (`for_race`, `weapon_model`, `for_unit`, `for_tribe`, `for_beast`, `for_mount`) checks it first, then the generated set in `models/gen` (`held.json`: what each person holds; `mounts.json`: each mount's saddle height; your converted mounts list theirs in `models/own/mounts.json`), then free animated animals in `models/free` (Quaternius CC0: livestock, wild herds, dinosaurs, the Raptor mount; `tools/blender/make_free_animals.py`) (docs/ART_SWAP.md). Units of faction `Animal` are `civilian`: they never fight and run from whoever hurts them (`_calm_left`); villages keep some as livestock (`Village.LIVESTOCK`). Two-handed weapons (`CharacterModel.TWO_HANDED`, by file name) swap in two-handed animations (`TWO_HANDED_ANIMS`) and pull the left hand onto the handle with a `TwoBoneIK3D` (target: the point of the handle nearest the left shoulder, each frame). `BeastModel` (extends CharacterModel) plays an animal's own clips (Idle, Walk, Gallop, Attack, HitReact, Death, Jump; a model with only a run clip walks on it at `WALK_ON_RUN` speed) behind the same play() names, or moves unanimated models in code (bob, lunge, flinch, fall); `ModelRegistry.BEASTS` lists them. `ModelRegistry` maps races, weapons and units to files; real art replaces entries there (later: the sheets' `model_file` columns). The player picks animations from its state each frame; enemies play the wind-up chop so the hit lands as the telegraph ends.

## Water

`ValleyTerrain` owns the water (banks: `RIVER_BANK` 20 m, `BANK_SLOPE` 5; per-seed `RIVER_SHIFT`, noise meanders `RIVER_NOISE`, irregular lake shores `shore_radius` / `LAKE_WOBBLE`): `RIVERS` (each drawn along one axis: "x" rivers cross the valley, the "z" main river runs its length; each with `rapids` and `bridges` positions), `BRANCHES` (side rivers off the main river, each ending in a fed lake at river level), `LAKES` / `DEEP_LAKES`, `waterfalls`, `hamlets` (riverside settlement spots). `_river_at(river, x, z)` gives the distance from a river's middle (round past its ends), used for carving the banks, lake and hamlet placement and the rapids' current. `water_surface(x, z)` (lake surface, `WATER_Y` for rivers and low ground, -INF if dry) and `current_push(x, z, race)` (rapids and deep lakes push everyone except `SWIMMER_RACE`, the Frogfolk). A **giant lake** (`GIANT_LAKE`, not deep) has an island (`lake.island`, raised in `_raw_height`) holding a castle landmark; two **floating islands** (`sky_islands`: pos, radius, top, steps) hold `SKY_ISLANDS` landmarks (with a `y`), reached by stepping stones (`STEP_RISE`, `STEP_GAP`); `stand_height(x, z, from_y)` is the island top when you're up there, else the ground (used by world-event units, thrown and pinned enemies, flyers). `ValleyWorld._build_sky_islands` builds them (your `sky_island.glb` / `sky_stone.glb` in models/own/world win). `Combat.water` points at it in the valley (null in the arena). The Player swims when deeper than `SWIM_ENTER` (floats, no attacks; the current's direction is locked when you enter so it always drives you back); enemies float and slow down. `ValleyWorld` builds bridges (box decks with colliders), lake discs, the rapids surface, waterfall sheets and spray (a scrolling-stripe water shader).

## Effects

`ElementFx` gives every damage type its look (`STYLES`: colors, particle shape, motion). `Combat._feedback` calls `hit()` on every non-status hit (bigger for heavy hits and crits), `Combat.deal` calls `kill()` on elemental kills, statuses add an `aura()` to the unit while they last (`StatusEffects.auras`, sized by `aura_size`), abilities and godly gift blasts use `area()`, shots use `beam()` (lightning zig-zags), projectiles carry a `trail()`. At most `HITS_PER_FRAME` hit bursts per frame. All particles are CPUParticles3D built in code: swap the table for hand-made effects later.

**Combat feel plan, part 1** (user, 2026-11-18; `docs/COMBAT_FEEL_PLAN.md`): `Vfx.blood` throws round drops scaled by `gore_amount` (the gore slider; 0 = none); `Combat._feedback` adds `Vfx.blood_stains` (splashes raycast onto the ground ahead of the blow, stretched its way, kept `blood_stain_seconds`, oldest gone past `blood_stain_max`) and on a kill a `blood_kill_drops` cloud; hits on `Enemy.wears_metal()` units (by role: Melee, Cavalry, Champion; not barbarians or beasts) throw `Vfx.sparks`, and a killing blow knocks off a `Vfx.armor_piece` (a RigidBody3D helmet or plate, at most `armor_pieces_max`). `CameraRig.kick(dir, amount)` pushes the view toward your blows (`camera_kick`, double for heavy). `CharacterModel.push_ragdoll` spins a launched body end over end (`launch_spin`). `SwingTrail` (combat/swing_trail.gd, made by `Player._update_animation`) draws a ribbon from the weapon's grip to `weapon_tip()` during LIGHT and HEAVY swings, steel tinted `swing_trail_tint` toward your element. **Part 2:** melee light attacks are `light_speed_mult` (1.25) faster (`Player._light_duration`; sidearms unchanged); `Player.charge_shown()` drives the HUD's charge bar under the crosshair (`Hud._charge`, red, orange, yellow by thirds). **Part 3:** `WeaponMoveset.launch_scale()` (Heavy 1.3, Polearm 1.1, Blade/Ranged/Caster 1.0, Twin 0.8) multiplies your launches in `Enemy.launch_from_hit`; spell bolts (`fire_projectile` with `elemental`) freeze `hitstop_heavy` and shake, and `launch_bolt_scale` is 1. ## Enemy shots, being stuck, ragdolls

- **Shots:** `Enemy` shooters (attack range > 5 m) lock `_aim_at` `ranged_aim_lock` seconds before firing, then `_shoot` fires a `Projectile` (`hostile` = hits you and the "allies" group). Magic-role shots get `homing` + `pull_deg`; your own shots never home: they go where the crosshair is, arrows aimed high enough to drop onto it (`Player.aim_direction`), with the camera over your right shoulder (`aim_shoulder_offset`) and a gold reticle for bows and spell weapons.
- **Stuck:** `Enemy._check_stuck` side-steps (`_detour`) when a unit barely moves while trying to. `Player._unstick` pops you back above the valley ground; the Movement leap follows `Player.floor_below`.
- **Ragdolls:** `CharacterModel.ragdoll(push)` adds a `PhysicalBoneSimulator3D` with capsule `PhysicalBone3D`s (`RAGDOLL_BONES`, layer 6, world mask) and starts it; `MAX_RAGDOLLS` at once; `sink_ragdoll()` drops it through the ground before removal. The skeleton only takes the bodies' pose while drawing, so tests check the bodies.

## Mounts

`Mount` (`scripts/player/mount.gd`, a child of the Player): on foot in the valley it keeps a **companion** (`Enemy.setup_companion`: a FRIENDLY unit with the mount's look and health, half its charge damage per butt, `COMPANION_LEASH`); riding puts it away, its death starts `THROWN_COOLDOWN`. The race's Mounts row, `ride()` / `dismount()` / `toggle()` (X, via `PlayerIntent.mount`), `speed()` (sheet x `mount_speed_scale`), `absorb()` (hits land on its hp; at 0 you're thrown, `THROWN_COOLDOWN`), `_trample()` while sprinting; at a full gallop it also calls `Enemy.throw()`: people go ragdoll (`CharacterModel.ragdoll` / `push_ragdoll` / `unragdoll`), animals fly whole; a sudden speed loss (`throw_impact_min`) is an impact (`throw_impact_damage` per m/s lost); alive, they get up (`Spawn_Ground`). The Player's `_move` uses mount speeds and no jump/slide while riding, wider turns, no heavy attack; dodge dismounts. The model is a `BeastModel` from `ModelRegistry.for_mount`; the rider is raised to the `saddle` height.

## The valley (3D)

- **Size and layout:** `ValleyTerrain.HALF_W` x `HALF_L` (1140 x 2280 m half-sizes: the first 1000 x 2000 x `SCALE` 1.14; region centers from the sheet and river/bridge positions are scaled by `SCALE` in code), a rounded rectangle (`shape_q`: 0 at the center, 1 at the edge). **`ValleyLayout`** swaps your race's home region with the south end (the `player_home` row) for the run (`RunState.flags.swap`, set by `ValleyGenerator.generate`, restored by `Run.resume`); `ValleyTerrain.region_center` and `ValleyGenerator.neighbors` follow the swap. Count Fuckula's tribe holds whatever region stands at `ValleyLayout.north()`.
- **Teams** (`Enemy.Team`): every unit is HOSTILE ("enemies" group, layer 3), FRIENDLY ("allies", layer 4) or NEUTRAL ("villagers", layer 4). Hostile units target you or your warriors (`_pick_target`); friendly units target the nearest enemy and otherwise follow you. Attack tokens: `max_attackers` on you, `CrowdDirector.ALLY_ATTACKERS` on each warrior. Friendly warriors get `ally_health_mult` x their sheet health. XP comes from every hostile kill, whoever lands it.
- **Village** (`village.gd`): buildings from `ValleyWorld.build_village`, villagers (Tribe Members, `civilian`, wander and flee battles) and guards (Army Units at posts). `defend()` / `turn_hostile()` / `calm()` / `corrupt()`; in a fight villagers run to the nearest house and hide (`Enemy.hide_at`, hidden) until `calm()`. Far villages go dormant (`Enemy.set_dormant`: processing off, hidden, out of the `CrowdDirector` list, so hundreds of sleeping units cost nothing).
- **Meeting a tribe** (user, 2026-11-16: tribes only join, never conquered): `Valley._check_villages` -> `meet(race)` runs the tribe's `tm_<race>` by its welcome (`ValleyGenerator.WELCOMES`, about a quarter each): a raid (`start_village_battle(race)`, guards on your side), a champion's challenge at the village, a camp to clear out in the valley, or a siege (`tm.siege`: `siege_prep_seconds` to get ready or F, then `start_village_battle(race, from)` with the attackers gathering outside the far gate, `_far_gate`, reinforcements from there too). - **Wilds** (`wilds.gd`): `GROUPS` roaming groups from the `PACKS` table (biome -> packs of Barbarians & Beasts units). Units are HOSTILE with `aggro_radius` (notice you), `roam_radius` (wander) and a leash (give up the chase). `update()` sleeps far groups and respawns wiped-out ones `RESPAWN_AWAY` from you after `RESPAWN_SECONDS`. `WATER_GROUPS` more come from `WATER_PACKS` (water monsters) at `find_water_spot` (rivers and lakes, `CLEAR_BRIDGE` from bridges). - **Battle size:** `EncounterSystem.plan(state, id, friendlies)` aims enemy health at `hp_budget` = (`battle_player_dps` x level/godly gift growth + `battle_ally_dps` x friendlies^`battle_ally_scaling`) x `target_minutes`, as `wave_count` (3) waves each `wave_growth` (1.5x) bigger, the first kept between `first_wave_min` and `first_wave_max`; then `plan.boss` (a mini boss, made tougher by `mini_boss_hp_mult`, or the champion) arrives once the last wave is down (`WaveDirector.boss`, `boss_started`). Who fights (`_party`): a tribe's own race, or for other fights one other race's army or a bandit party; waves hold unit tokens, `"unit_id@race"` = a barbarian in that race's look (`Enemy.look_race`, `ModelRegistry.for_race_role`). Allies add health, not enemies: waves are sized from `hp_budget(..., 0)` and `plan.health_mult` (budget with your warriors / budget alone) multiplies every hostile unit's health (`WaveDirector.health_mult` -> `Enemy.health_mult`). `WaveDirector` tracks its own `members` and sends the next wave when `wave_overlap` of the current one is left. Recalibrate with `tools\battle_sim.bat` (an autopilot plays real fights) after big combat changes; `-- --seed=N --only=<encounter_id>` measures one fight on one seed (run several seeds: single runs are noisy).

In the final battle `muster` adds the veterans and giant champions to your line and catapults behind it (a stone every `catapult_interval`), and `intervene` (when the lines clash) drops `meteors_per_volley` meteors per volley bought. Numbers in the tuning (`catapult_*`, `meteor_*`).
- **Wings** (user, 2026-11-05): winged people (angel, bird and owl races, the harpy, the gargoyle) are built with two bones per wing (`make_characters.py` `WING_NAMES`). `CharacterModel._beat_wings` turns them by the little demon's wing beat (read once from `beast_little_demon.glb`, `flap_curve`) while `flapping`: always for flying units, in the air for the player.
- **Mount throws** (user, 2026-11-05): a trample knocks down but never launches (`no_launch`); only after `mount_throw_after` seconds at a full gallop (`Mount._gallop_time`) are enemies thrown.
- **EncounterSite**: encounters that aren't a tribe's choice as places (no beams): a camp, den, shrine, banners or stronghold from `ValleyWorld` props, a sign seen within `SIGN_RANGE`, and a few of the fight's own units standing there (NEUTRAL `sentries`). `AUTO` encounters (camps, war parties, taken shrines, border raids) start when you come within `AUTO_RANGE` (`Valley._update_sites`, `EncounterSite.spotted`); crouched, only when a sentry would notice you (`Enemy._notice_distance`, their `aggro_radius` = `AUTO_RANGE`). Their sentries are STRIKABLE: a blow on one before the fight kills it (`Valley._on_hit_landed`), and all of them down clears the camp without the battle (`cleared_quietly` -> `_clear_quietly`). The rest wait for F. The fight's waves replace the sentries. **WorldEvents** (`valley/world_events.gd`, made in `Valley._begin`): caravans, rituals, one event per bridge (troll, camp, shop, quest) and a quest-giver per hamlet, all real units and props; `update()` wakes them near you and finishes them (supplies, `Diplomacy.change_reputation`, a godly-gift offer via the `event_gift` choice); `prompt()` / `interact()` / `on_choice()` handle F and the HUD choices. Finished ids go to `RunState.flags.events_done`; each event rolls its own dice (seed + its id) and the tribe list includes fallen tribes, so an id means the same event in every load of the run. **ValleyHud**: region name, Count Fuckula's clock, the F prompt, the map (M/Tab, `ValleyMap`) and the pause menu (Esc).

## The world

- **ValleyGenerator**: places a tribe in every region but yours (`FULL_TRIBES` 9; prefers each race's home region), corrupts one (the evil tribe), imprisons its patron god, picks wild gods, and places each tribe's join event, wild-god encounters, barbarian camps and the final battle. Region borders and the home region come from the Valley Regions sheet (`neighbors`, `player_home`).
- **The horde** (`EvilTribe.HORDE`): Count Fuckula's tribe is not a race. `GameData.race("horde")` returns `HORDE_ROW` (its name); its units are the Barbarians & Beasts rows of faction "Horde" (`EncounterSystem.army_rows`, also used for the guards of every village he holds); `HORDE_BOSS` (the Evil Dragon) ends the final battle. `ValleyGenerator` gives it the north end and a captive god no tribe worships.
- **EvilTribe**: every `advance_interval_min` x `evil_push_scale` (1.75; scaled by run length) pushes into a neighboring region. A living tribe there triggers a border attack: a `border_<region>` Border Defense encounter with a `border_defense_seconds` window (300 s, any run length; valley time only); otherwise, or if unanswered, the region and tribe are corrupted. The home region is never attacked.
- **Diplomacy**: reputation -> neutral / friendly (30) / allied (70). Missions raise it. Allies join the final battle. No subjugation (user, 2026-11-16).
- **EncounterSystem**: `plan()` turns an encounter into waves of unit ids (deterministic per seed); `finish()` applies results and returns a god to offer.
- **Void**: the evil tribe's status on the player; 5 stacks suppress godly gifts for 3 s; dodging removes a stack; Light/Stone hits on corrupted enemies clear it.

## Saving

`SaveSystem` writes `user://run_save.json` (atomic temp-file swap). `Run.checkpoint(label)` after encounters, border attacks and region falls, and on window close (it stores HP and the dice state too, so a reload doesn't replay the same rolls). `Run.resume()` loads and deletes the save, so a save can never undo a death. The profile is `user://profile.json`.

## Controls

## The living valley (2026-11-03)
- `Wilds.PACKS` lists what roams each biome. Its first three packs are that biome's own animals; `Wilds.MONSTERS` is its rare monster. `_land_pack` mixes a second kind into hostile groups and sometimes puts the monster in charge. Beast encounters draw on the same lists (`EncounterSystem._party`).
- Who attacks: faction "Animal" never does (it runs when hurt). Faction "Neutral" (`Enemy.peaceful`) does once hit; `WaveDirector.spawn` turns that off in fights.
- Moving: rig_type starting "Water" stays in deep water (`water_bound`); starting "Flying" (`flying`) holds `fly_height` above the ground and dives at a target within 25 m.
- `Wilds._discover`: first time near a kind of creature = XP (`Arena.give_xp`), codex entry `creature:<id>`, saved in `flags.discovered`. No message (user, 2026-11-05).
- While a unit is limp its root follows its body (`Enemy._update_throw`), so everything that aims at `global_position` finds the body. Its capsule is off until it gets up.
- Villages (`ValleyWorld.build_village`): square and market, three rings of buildings between `ROADS` roads (`_between_roads`), farm land (`YARD`), walls with gates (`_build_walls`). Parts come from `tools/blender/make_village_parts.py`. The walls have an opening every few sections because units have no pathfinding. The buildings root's `spots` meta tells `Village` where people work and guards stand.
- `Run.practice`: the Practice arena's run never saves, never deletes the real save, never touches the profile.

## Changes of 2026-11-09
- **Fights:** a fight is one battle. `Arena._one_battle` pools the plan's waves into `battle_size` enemies and scales their health to match. `WaveDirector` sends the boss in at `boss_when_left`, and `_open_ground` keeps spawns off roofs, trees and water.
- **Buildings break:** `Breakable.attach_building` (solid buildings and walls, from `ValleyWorld._building`). The first blow cuts the building into pieces of about `CHUNK` (`_cut_up`), each with its own box. A small building comes down after `_hits_left` blows; a big one (wider than `BIG`) only loses the pieces near the blow. Blows come from `smash_near`, i.e. your swings, abilities, shots (`Projectile`), blasts (`Combat.area`) and the knife. Not saved.
- **Towers:** `LootSpots.route(spot)` gives the plain climb, its mirror image, or `INSIDE` (the hollow tower's steps). The variant is in the landmark (`ValleyTerrain.TOWER_VARIANTS`); hollow towers use the `tower_<art>_hollow` models.
- **Roads:** `ValleyWorld._build_roads` lays one dirt mesh along `terrain.roads`.
- **First person:** `CameraRig.first_person` (T) hides `target.visual`, and `Mount._camera` leaves the arm alone while it's on.
- **Spells:** `Player.fire_projectile` starts magic at `CharacterModel.weapon_tip()`.
